# 3 Pillars of Clear Explanation

_Explanation requires combining clarity of purpose, simplified language, and essential information for the audience._

## Breakdown

- High-impact explanation begins with establishing absolute clarity of purpose.
- Defined purposes allow for the precise identification of essential information.
- Essential information necessitates using simplified language to remove comprehension obstacles.
