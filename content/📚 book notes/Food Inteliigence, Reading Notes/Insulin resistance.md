# Insulin resistance explained


Insulin resistance is when your cells stop responding well to insulin, so your body needs more of it to move glucose out of the blood.  

It happens when cells are chronically exposed to high insulin levels, often driven by excess calorie intake, abdominal fat, inactivity, stress, or disrupted sleep.  

Its outcomes include 
* higher blood sugar, 
* increased fat storage, 
* inflammation, 
* difficulty losing weight, 
* and over time a rising risk of type 2 diabetes and heart disease.
