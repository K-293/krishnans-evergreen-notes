# Life History Theory


Every organism must divide energy between 
* growth, 
* survival, and 
* reproduction.  

This creates **tradeoffs** because energy spent on one task cannot be used for another.  

Environments shape which tradeoffs make sense.  

In harsh or unpredictable settings, fast strategies evolve.  
* Individuals grow quickly, mature early, and reproduce sooner.  
* They have more offspring with less investment in each.  

In stable and safe settings, slow strategies evolve.  
* Individuals grow longer, mature later, and reproduce later.  
* They have fewer offspring with higher investment in each.  

These patterns help explain why species differ in lifespan, size, and parenting.
