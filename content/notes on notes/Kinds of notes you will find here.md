This garden contains different kinds of notes, often overlapping.

1. Some notes are quick captures. Short thoughts, questions, quotes, or fragments written to avoid losing an idea. I call them zettels or fleeting notes
2. Some notes are working-notes. Messy explorations where I try to understand a topic, map connections, or think something through.
3. Some notes are evergreen-notes. Ideas that have stabilized enough to be useful over time, though they may still change.
4. Some notes are references. Pointers to books, articles, tools, or external resources with light commentary.
