Your understanding, judgment, and taste cannot be outsourced to a model.

Because models generate patterns based on past data. While,
* understanding grows from sustained engagement with problems over time.
* Judgment comes from seeing consequences play out. 
* Taste forms through repeated choices, failures, and refinements that are specific to you.

> A model can assist thinking, but it cannot accumulate lived context or responsibility. That accumulation is what personal knowledge is for.

But most personal knowledge systems fail because ideas are captured but never revisited, connected, or allowed to evolve. 

A digital garden treats notes as living things rather than finished artefacts. 

By keeping [[Kinds of notes you will find here|thoughts public, loosely structured, and continuously revisable]], this setup encourages reflection, cross linking, and long term learning instead of static note hoarding.
