# About this Vault

This is my public Obsidian vault.

I use it to capture thoughts as I work through them. Most of what you’ll see here is unfinished, exploratory, and sometimes messy.

These notes exist to help me think clearly, notice patterns, and learn over time. They are not meant to be authoritative or complete.

Some ideas will change, some will remain rough, and some may be wrong. That’s expected.

You’re welcome to read along if it’s useful.
